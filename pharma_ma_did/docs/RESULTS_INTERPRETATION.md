# Results interpretation matrix (applied to the provisional estimates)

Observed provisional pattern: **finance relatively stronger** (ROA differential +23.6 pp,
insignificant) and **innovation roughly flat** (patent differential +0.7 families / +0.2 PPML,
insignificant) for innovation-driven vs alternative-rationale deals.

- This *could* be consistent with large innovation-driven acquirers integrating small targets
  without disrupting their own patent output while consolidating financially. It is **equally**
  consistent with firm-size confounding (innovation group = large caps).
- The negative raw patent change for the innovation group (−2.5 vs −0.05 families) is in the
  H1-predicted direction descriptively, but the modelled differential is ~0 and imprecise.
- **Do not** read the insignificant coefficients as "no effect": the CIs include large effects
  in both directions (ROA CI −11 to +58 pp). This is low power, not equivalence.
- **Do not** attribute the ROA pattern to acquisition classification without addressing the
  pre-trend divergence and size imbalance first.

Cautious phrasings to use in the thesis: "the estimate indicates…", "consistent with…",
"under the parallel-trends assumption…", "the confidence interval includes…", "should be
interpreted cautiously because…". Avoid "proves", "caused", "no effect", and any claim that
innovation-driven acquisitions improve performance in absolute terms.
