# Chapter 5 — Discussion bridge

## From estimates to interpretation
The empirical stage compared two categories of **completed** European pharmaceutical M&A:
innovation-driven transactions (targets bringing a material pre-deal pipeline, platform, or
R&D capability) and alternative-rationale transactions (scale, geography, mature products,
services, or efficiency). The differential DiD estimates were:

- **Innovation output (H1):** differential ≈ **0** (−0.46 families; wild-p 0.82), confirmed
  by PPML and IHS. Over three post-completion years, innovation-driven acquirers show **no
  additional (nor reduced) patent-family output** relative to other acquirers.
- **Financial performance (H2):** differential **+10.8 pp ROA** in point estimate but
  **insignificant** (wild-p 0.40) and **driven by a few small, volatile acquirers**.

## Consistency with the literature's mechanisms (framing only)
This pattern is *consistent with*, but does not identify, several mechanisms discussed in
the innovation-M&A literature:
- **Innovation–finance divergence / consolidation:** near-term financial gains without a
  measured innovation dividend can reflect cost consolidation, removal of duplicate R&D
  projects, or faster financial than scientific synergies.
- **Development lags:** patent-family counts by earliest priority year may not register
  acquired late-stage pipeline value within a three-year window; the innovation output
  metric is a **quantity**, not a **value**, measure.
- **Classification and coverage attenuation:** the comparison group demonstrably contained
  true innovation deals (false negatives), which **attenuates** any true contrast toward
  zero — a plausible partial explanation for the H1 null.

The regressions **do not independently identify** which mechanism operates, and the
estimates are too imprecise to adjudicate. No causal or managerial-motive claim is made.

## What would move this from associational to confirmatory
1. **Archived primary evidence** (company announcements, target filings) to reduce
   classification error, especially resolving the false negatives now sitting in the
   comparison group.
2. **Broader outcome coverage** — Bloomberg actuals and Lens exports for more of the
   eligible 100%/majority deals — to raise the acquirer-cluster count above the small-sample
   regime and support matched/weighted specifications.
3. **Value-weighted innovation outcomes** (cohort-normalised citations, pipeline-stage
   milestones) alongside raw family counts.
4. **Cohort-stratified / stacked event studies** once cell sizes permit, to guard against
   staggered-timing bias.
