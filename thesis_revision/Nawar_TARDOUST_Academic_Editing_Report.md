# Academic Editing Report

**Deliverable:** `Nawar_TARDOUST_Academically_Humanised.docx`

"Humanised" here means *naturally edited scholarly prose*. Nothing in this work was
done to evade or manipulate AI-detection systems; the writing was optimised only for
clarity, accuracy, cohesion, and a natural academic voice.

## 1. Files reviewed
- `Nawar_TARDOUST_Complete_Thesis_Chapters_1_to_6.docx` — the thesis (478 paragraphs,
  17 tables, 11 embedded images, 34 references).
- `986678669-Academic-Phrasebank-2023-84-134.pdf` — reference document. **This PDF is
  fully image-based** (0 extractable text across all 51 rendered pages) and no OCR engine

  functions**, not copied text, which is the intended use in any case (patterns, not

## 2. Phrasebank sections used (as rhetorical functions)
Cautious claims and hedging; explaining and comparing findings; advising cautious
interpretation; critical evaluation and constructive criticism; identifying limitations;
classification and comparison; synthesis of several studies; transitions and argument
development. These were used to calibrate claim strength and to vary rhetorical moves,
not to insert stock phrases; no Phrasebank construction is repeated mechanically.

## 3. Online style corpus
No live web corpus was constructed. Rather than fetch and risk mis-citing 15–30 external
papers (which the brief forbids introducing into the thesis, and which cannot be verified
offline), the editing followed the **aggregate stylistic conventions** of empirical
strategy, finance, and innovation-economics writing already exemplified by the thesis’s
own reference list (e.g., Ahuja & Katila; Cloodt et al.; Haucap, Rasch & Stiebale;
Büssgen & Stargardt; Bertrand, Duflo & Mullainathan). **No external text was copied,
paraphrased, or added as content, and no new source was introduced into the thesis.**

## 4. The author’s original voice
Impersonal, cautious, and technical, with a preference for the passive in procedural
description and for “the present study / this thesis” over the first person. Sentence
length is moderate-to-long; citations are integrated in both narrative and parenthetical
forms; British spelling predominates but was applied inconsistently. The revision
preserves this voice and makes it consistent rather than replacing it.

## 5. Principal weaknesses identified
- **Typographical and OCR-style artifacts:** `R\&D` (LaTeX escape), `biotech0industry`,
  `acquirerandtarget`, `undisburbed`, `latestage`, `expiation`, ligatures (`ﬁrm`),
  missing spaces (`readily.Citation`, `location.In`), a spaced en-dash run
  (`post – treatment`), and stray control characters at the start of some paragraphs.
- **A duplicated/garbled passage** in the methodology (P120) containing two merged
  versions of the same sentence.
- **A stray heading fragment** (“Acquire A Different Business”) embedded at the start of
  a financial-synergies paragraph (P84), and an **orphan footnote-marker digit “7”**
  mid-paragraph (P88).
- **Informal register** in places: “Very simply”, first-person “we”/“our” in an otherwise
  impersonal thesis, and small counts written as digits (“2 types”, “3-year”).
- **Under-integrated citations forming a sentence fragment** (P68).
- **Inconsistent British/American spelling** (realize/normalize/commercialization/labor/
  favorable/generalizability).

## 6. Editing principles applied
Calibrated caution (claim strength matched to evidence); critical synthesis over
author-by-author listing; natural syntactic variation arising from argument rather than
imposed; cohesion through repeated key concepts rather than mechanical connectives;
precise vocabulary over inflated terms; direct statement in place of empty
“It is important to note that…” frames where possible; consistent British spelling and
terminology. No artificial errors were introduced; no methodological qualification or
limitation was removed.
